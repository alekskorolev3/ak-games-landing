# Shape brief — AK Games landing page

## 1. Job and audience
Investors and partners arrive at ak.games evaluating a partnership or investment. They need to quickly assess credibility, track record, and opportunity, then decide to engage. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

## 2. Outcome and proof
Primary task: view the portfolio (games + track record) and make contact. Success = a qualified meeting request or partnership inquiry via the primary action. Proof: the studio's real, confirmed facts presented concretely (currently placeholders for exact figures); nothing fabricated. Product truth on display: a small studio whose quality/craft is the differentiator.

## 3. Selected direction
**The category standard, played straight** at top-iGaming-studio craft level (Pragmatic Play, Play'n GO, Hacksaw Gaming as the bar). Conventional single-page investor layout: nav → hero (name, one-line offer, primary CTA) → portfolio/games grid → studio + track record → contact. Professional, premium, trustworthy. No irony, no smuggled quirk.

## 4. Scope and boundaries
- Single landing page; Vue/Nuxt; responsive (desktop + mobile).
- Fidelity: production-ready.
- Anti-goals: no invented stats/testimonials/claims; no casino-glam clichés or neon theatrics; no animation for its own sake; not a multi-step funnel.

## 5. States and ranges
- Portfolio: 1–8 games realistically (count placeholder until supplied).
- States: portfolio thumbnail loading/empty; contact form success/error/validation.
- Real content where available; everything unconfirmed ships as a clearly-marked placeholder.

## 6. Interaction and layout
Standard single-page hierarchy with a strong hero (name, one-line offer, primary CTA above the fold), a portfolio grid with restrained hover states, a track-record section, and a contact form. Grid collapses to one column on mobile; CTA stays reachable without deep scrolling. Smooth scroll, subtle transitions. Intent only — exact CSS is the build's concern.

## 7. Constraints and open decisions
- Vue/Nuxt; web; logo preserved.
- Palette: open — to be established in new-work as a professional iGaming palette (no locked colors).
- Pending user supply: exact figures (founded, team, games shipped, locations, milestones), portfolio art/thumbnails.
- Open: contact channel (form vs. email), hosting/deploy target.
